    Don’t make an emergency during an emergency
    • Roof rakes: work from the ground, never from a ladder, and not alone. Find the power line to the house first and stay far from it; a metal rake carries current. Stand clear of the snow as it comes down.
    • Ladders: ice on the rungs, ice on your boots. Stay off them if you can. If you must climb, have someone hold it.
    • Chainsaws: a storm is no time to learn. Never cut a tree that is touching a wire.
    • Flame: never leave a candle alone. Camp stoves and grills stay outdoors.
